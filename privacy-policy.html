This Privacy Policy explains how PeramS Academy (“we”, “our”, or “us”) collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use the PeramS Academy mobile application.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Account information: name, email address, and account/user identifiers provided through Google Sign-In.
  • App activity: course access, learning progress, tests, quiz activity, and other interactions with the app where these features are enabled.
  • Support information: information you voluntarily provide when you contact us for support or account deletion.
  • Purchase information: transaction or entitlement information needed to provide paid digital courses or services, where purchases are enabled. Payment card details are not stored by PeramS Academy when payment processing is handled by an authorized payment provider or Google Play.
  • AI feature input: questions or prompts submitted to AI-powered learning or doubt-solving features, where such features are enabled.
  • Technical information: limited device, diagnostic, security, and usage information that may be processed by service providers used to operate and secure the app.

2. How We Use Information

  • To create and manage user accounts.
  • To provide courses, tests, quizzes, study materials, live/online learning features, and other educational services.
  • To maintain learning progress and account entitlements.
  • To respond to support requests and account deletion requests.
  • To prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents.
  • To improve app reliability, performance, and educational features.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by trusted service providers that help us operate the app, such as authentication, cloud hosting, analytics/diagnostics, payment processing, and AI service providers where those features are used. We may also disclose information when required by law or to protect users, our services, or legal rights.

4. Google Sign-In

PeramS Academy may use Google Sign-In for authentication. Google may provide basic account information such as your name, email address, and account identifier according to the permissions you approve. PeramS Academy does not receive your Google account password.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards to protect user information. Data transmitted between the app and online services is intended to use encrypted connections such as HTTPS/TLS.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, satisfy legal or accounting obligations, resolve disputes, prevent fraud, and enforce agreements. Information that no longer needs to be retained will be deleted or anonymized where appropriate.
