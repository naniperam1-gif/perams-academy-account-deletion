Request Account Deletion

This page allows PeramS Academy users to request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email from the same email address used to sign in to PeramS Academy.
  2. Use the subject: PeramS Academy – Account Deletion Request.
  3. Include the email address associated with your PeramS Academy account.
  4. We may contact you only if additional verification is required to protect your account.
Before publishing: Replace peramsacademy@gmail.com below with your real support email address.
Request Account Deletion

Data that will be deleted

After a verified deletion request, PeramS Academy will delete data associated with the account where applicable, including:

  • Account profile information such as name and email address.
  • Account identifiers and authentication-related account records controlled by PeramS Academy.
  • App activity or learning-related records linked to the account, where deletion is technically and legally permitted.

Data that may be retained

Some information may be retained when required for legal, security, fraud-prevention, accounting, dispute-resolution, or regulatory obligations. Any retained information will be kept only for the period required for those purposes and then deleted or anonymized.

Important note about Google Sign-In

Deleting your PeramS Academy account does not delete your Google Account. It only deletes the PeramS Academy account and associated data controlled by PeramS Academy.

Processing time

Verified deletion requests will be processed as soon as reasonably possible. Where applicable, users will be informed if additional verification or legally required retention applies.
